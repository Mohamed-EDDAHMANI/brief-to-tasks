# Output template

Use these headings, in this order. Drop a heading that has nothing to say.

```
## Size
Small | Medium | Large, and why in one line.

## What I understood
One sentence.
Assumptions (mark the unsure ones with "(not sure)"):
- ...

## Questions
1. <question>. If no answer, I assume <default>.
(Small: at most 2. Medium: at most 5. Large: at most 8. Biggest impact first.)

## Tasks
1. <verb ...>. Done when <check>.
2. ⚠ <risky task>. Done when <check>. Risk: <why>. Depends on question <n>.
(Small: 3 to 5. Medium: 5 to 8. Large: 6 to 8 at module level; they are split once the questions are answered.)

## Estimate
| Task | Range |
|---|---|
| 1 ... | 2 to 4 h |
| **Total** | **X to Y h, about A to B days** |

Depending on the answers:
- <answer that changes the estimate>: add <range>.

Left out: design, deployment, review, client feedback, meetings (list only what applies).
Confidence: low | medium | high. <which question raises it>
These numbers are guesses from the information given, not a delivery date.

## Risks
- <hidden work, wrong order or contradiction found by the skeptic, with its effect>

## Cut list
Remove first, in this order:
1. ...
The smallest useful version: ...

## Reply to the client
<short message with the 5 most important questions at most, plain words>
```
